## What it is
One licensed kitchen in Aruba, five delivery-only restaurants. To customers, **Pidi is a delivery app, like Uber Eats** (Pidi is Papiamento for "to order"; it was called Order Aruba until 10 Oct 2026), and **each restaurant is its own business** (Victor, 9 Oct: "people also cannot know it's all the same kitchen, they really need to believe each restaurant is their own business").
The name lives in one place: `shared/site.json` `"name": "Pidi"`. Code reads `SITE.name`; static files that carry it (home title and og:title, the wordmark's alt text, the 404 link, `manifest.webmanifest`) are rewritten from it by `build/seo.py`, and `--check` fails when they drift.
The home page lists the restaurants; each restaurant page is its own shop. One order can mix restaurants (`"mix": true` in site.json), said as the app's perk ("Order from several restaurants at once. You pay ƒ5 delivery once."). Delivery only: no pickup, no dine-in.
**Ordering is moving into the app** (this branch, `pidi-site`, builds it step by step from the design brief "Lights On" and the build contract, `/home/user/research_notes/pidi-design/brief.md` and `BUILD_CONTRACT.md`, outside the repo): the checkout sends the order with `Pidi.rpc('pidi_place_order')` (`shared/pidi.js`) and the customer then watches the live status page `order/#t=<token>` (Received, Cooking, On the way, Delivered). WhatsApp (+297 747 7794) is for questions only. The checkout and status pages are still being built here: until they land, the restaurant pages still send the order on WhatsApp, so keep that flow working.
Static site, no framework, no build step. Hosted on GitHub Pages from this repo (`main` branch, root folder).

## Locked rules (Pidi, 10 Oct 2026; check every screen against them)
1. The name is **Pidi**, from `SITE.name`. "Order Aruba" never appears anywhere a customer can see or load.
2. Five restaurants, each its own business. Never say or hint they share a kitchen or an owner. Nothing a customer can load names an owner or a kitchen company (screens, `site.json`, language files, bank details, receipts), calls a restaurant "own", "partner", "sister" or "ours", or shows a cook's name.
3. Delivery only. Hours on screen: **"Late night from 10 PM"**. No closing time, no last-orders time, no "until 2 AM" anywhere (the permit isn't confirmed). `site.json` `hours` and `lastOrder` are logic only.
4. **ƒ24 food minimum** on the whole order (`"minFood": 2400`). One order can mix restaurants and pays **one delivery fee: the highest `fee` among its restaurants** (`"fee": 500` on each of the five; `deliveryFee` is only the fallback). A later restaurant with a higher fee carries `"fee": 1000`, never a `"partner"` flag (a flag would tell anyone reading the public file that the others are "own"). Show the fee before checkout. Never say "charged": nothing is charged on Pidi.
5. Pay **cash** ("I'll pay with ƒ__", the driver brings change; "We take cash in florins or US dollars") or **bank transfer** ("Waiting for your transfer"). No online payment, no tips. Transfer stays hidden in the new checkout until `site.json` has a `transfer` block from Victor, in a neutral name. No US dollar rate on screen until Victor gives one.
6. Delivery time **45–60 min**: `"etaMin": 45, "etaMax": 60`; the text is built from the numbers.
7. Dead simple, big photos, one step per screen, plain words, no codes. The order number is small print.
8. Every image shows exactly what its words say. No picture beats a wrong one.
9. Four languages (EN, PAP, NL, ES) for all screen text; dish names, descriptions and taglines stay English.
10. No paid tools or services. No status, rating or count that wasn't measured.
11. Times are Aruba time (UTC−4, `site.json` `utcOffset`), never the phone's.
`qa/check_site.py` and the human-touch `check.py` fail on the old name anywhere public, a closing time in screen text or the language files, and "partner", "own restaurant", "our restaurants", "sister" or "charged" in a customer file.

## Run locally
`python3 qa/check_site.py` in the repo folder (needs Python Playwright 1.56.0 and Pillow). It serves the repo itself for the run (`qa/local_server.py`), so there's no server to start or keep alive; `QA_BASE=<url>` checks another server instead. To click around yourself: `python3 qa/local_server.py` or `python3 -m http.server 8462 --bind 127.0.0.1`. The check also compares every dish's allergens with its recipe card in `ops/kitchen/kitchen-data.json`.
